What Milo wants to read.

Milo is looking for distinctive work with a strong voice, clear identity, and something memorable at its center. Expand any category below for more detail.

Milo is interested in horror/thriller/suspense that does more than simply frighten. Psychological horror, unsettling speculative work, strange premises, deeply human stakes, and stories where the horror means something are especially welcome.

Milo is drawn to fantasy with a strong narrative identity, memorable characters, unusual worldbuilding, and familiar conventions approached from an unexpected angle. Fantasy that has life breathed into every page is what she is looking for.

Miloe loves romance! Romance with sub genres in fantasy, horror, romcom, and dark. She is up for any romance but those are her favorites. One of her favorite books is Butcher and Blackbird by Brynne Weaver.
