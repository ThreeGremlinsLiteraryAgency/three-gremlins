So... What Is Three Gremlins?

Three Gremlins started with the sort of ideas that happen when creative people spend too much time together and nobody has the good sense to say no.

It became a shared universe, a collection of stories, businesses, characters, jokes, food, projects, and dreams built around one simple idea:

Life is already strange. We might as well make something wonderful out of it.

“Come for the chaos.
Stay for the stories.
We probably have snacks.”

Three Gremlins. One Questionable Plan.

Explore the stories, raid the bakery, browse the merch, and check back occasionally. We have absolutely no idea what we'll build next.
